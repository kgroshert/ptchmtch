    Echo

    Hear a short phrase, keep it in mind, sing it back

    No pitch is shown while you sing, so you judge by ear. The bar on the left shows how well each note has gone (red → yellow → green; first tries only; hover for details). The level goes up after 3 right rounds in a row and down after 2 wrong ones; a note counts as right within 50 cents, and as in tune within your tolerance (Settings).
